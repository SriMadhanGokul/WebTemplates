Our Story

Made with intention, worn with confidence

KAYRA began with a simple belief: well-made pieces should be easy to find, honestly priced and built to last. What started as a small studio collection has grown into a community of customers who care about how things are made as much as how they look.

Every product is designed in-house and produced with partners we know by name. We test fabrics and finishes for months, cut down on unnecessary packaging and keep our collections tight so that everything earns its place in your wardrobe.
