Appointments

How do I book an appointment?+

Book online in under a minute, call our helpline or visit reception. Same-day slots are often available.

Can I reschedule or cancel?+

Yes — reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 12 hours before your visit.

Do I need a referral?+

Most departments accept direct bookings without a referral.

Insurance & Billing

Which insurance do you accept?+

We work with all major insurers and handle claims directly for you.

What if I'm paying myself?+

Clear self-pay prices are shown before you book, with no hidden fees.

Your Visit

What should I bring?+

Bring photo ID, your insurance card, current medications and any previous reports.

Is there parking?+

Free patient parking is available on site, with accessible bays at the main entrance.
