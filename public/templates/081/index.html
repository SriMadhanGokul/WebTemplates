How long do lash extensions last?+

With infills every 2–3 weeks, extensions last as long as you like; a full set lasts through one natural lash cycle (6–8 weeks).

Will extensions damage my lashes?+

No — when applied by certified artists with the right weight, extensions are safe for your natural lashes.

How should I prepare?+

Arrive with clean, makeup-free eyes and avoid caffeine so you can relax during the appointment.

Can I wear mascara?+

We don't recommend it — extensions give definition on their own and mascara can shorten their life.
