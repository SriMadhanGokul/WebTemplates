Orders & Shipping

How long does delivery take?+

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days; express arrives in 1–2 days. You'll receive tracking as soon as your order ships.

Do you ship internationally?+

Yes, we ship to over 30 countries. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout so there are no surprises.

How can I track my order?+

Use the tracking link in your shipping email or log in to your account to see live updates.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?+

Return unworn items within 30 days for a full refund. Returns are free for all domestic orders.

How do exchanges work?+

Request an exchange from your account; we ship the new item as soon as your return is scanned.

When will I get my refund?+

Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of receiving your return.

Products & Care

How do I find my size?+

Every product page has a size guide with measurements. Our team is also happy to help over chat.

Are your products sustainable?+

We use certified and recycled materials wherever possible and plastic-free packaging.

Do you offer gift wrapping?+

Yes — add gift wrapping and a handwritten note at checkout.
