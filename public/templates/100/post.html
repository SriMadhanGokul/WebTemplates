The history of the high-top

CultureMar 12, 2026 · 5 min read

The history of the high-top — it's one of the topics our customers ask us about most. At STRIDE we've spent years learning what really works, so we put together this practical guide you can use straight away.

Start with the essentials

The biggest improvements usually come from getting the basics right. Before anything else, take a moment to think about what you actually need and how it fits into your everyday life. Small, considered choices add up quickly.

  • Invest in a few versatile staples before chasing trends
  • Check fabric composition and care labels before you buy
  • Choose fit over size — tailoring small tweaks makes a big difference
  • Store pieces properly so they keep their shape for years
“Good decisions are rarely complicated — they're simply made with care and a clear idea of what matters.”

How we approach it

Our team tests every idea in the real world before we recommend it. That means honest feedback, lots of iteration and a focus on quality over quantity. It's the same philosophy that shapes everything we make and do.

We also listen closely to our community. Many of the tips in this article came directly from customers who shared what worked for them — thank you for continuing to inspire us.

The takeaway

Keep it simple, stay curious and don't be afraid to experiment. If you have questions, our team is always happy to help — just get in touch.
