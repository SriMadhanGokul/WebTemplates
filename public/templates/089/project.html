The brief

Riverside Library is a public project where we were asked to create something both distinctive and deeply practical. Working closely with the client from the earliest workshops, our team shaped a solution that responds to real needs, real constraints and a clear ambition.

We began with research and on-site workshops, then developed concepts that balanced ambition with budget and timeline. Iterative reviews kept every stakeholder aligned as the design evolved.

The result

The finished project has been celebrated by its users and recognised by the industry — a result of honest collaboration, careful detailing and a shared commitment to quality.
