About Us

A calm space to move, breathe and grow

Life Yoga is a community studio offering yoga, mobility and mindful movement for every body. Our teachers are certified, experienced and genuinely passionate about helping you feel better on and off the mat.

Classes are small and welcoming, our studio is filled with natural light and our schedule runs from sunrise flows to evening restorative sessions.
