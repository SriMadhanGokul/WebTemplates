About Us

Helping you find a place to call home

Luxe Estates is an independent real-estate advisory built on local knowledge and long relationships. For over a decade we have guided buyers, sellers and investors through some of the city's most sought-after neighbourhoods.

Our advisors combine market data with an eye for design and lifestyle. Whether it is a first apartment or a waterfront estate, we handle the viewings, negotiations and paperwork so you can focus on the move.
